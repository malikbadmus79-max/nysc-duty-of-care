# Assumptions

Log of assumptions made in the analysis, referenced by ID wherever they are used in code, notebooks or reports.

| ID | Assumption | Reason | Used in | Sensitivity |
|----|------------|--------|---------|-------------|
| A01 | An incident is dated by the day the harm began (the day of abduction or attack), not the publication date of the report. Where only the month or year is known, the date is set to the first day of that month or year and `date_precision` records the precision. | News reports often appear one or more days after an event, and some reports give only an approximate date. | `nysc_safety.coding`; `docs/coding_protocol.md`; H2 travel-window test | Incidents with month or year precision are excluded from the H2 test, which needs day-level dates; the count excluded is reported with the result. |
| A02 | The number of corps members exposed in a state in a year equals the number sworn in at that state's orientation camp across all batches and streams of the year. | NYSC does not publish a time series of serving corps members by state; swearing-in figures are reported by state coordinators at each batch. | `nysc_safety.rates`; H3 and H4 | Excludes corps members redeployed into or out of a state after camp, and counts each corps member in the year of swearing-in only, although service runs across two calendar years. Rates are repeated with a two-year exposure window as a check. |
| A03 | Each geopolitical zone is assigned the states in the standard six-zone grouping, with the FCT in the North Central zone. | The zones have no statutory boundary list; the standard grouping is used in federal statistics and reporting. | `nysc_safety.coding.ZONES`; H1 | Only affects zone-level summaries; state-level results are unaffected. |
| A04 | A journey to camp, to the place of primary assignment or to passing-out is coded to the travel stage when the report places the person on a road, at a motor park or in a vehicle, and the report links the journey to NYSC service. | Reports rarely state the purpose of travel explicitly; the stage determines which policy controls apply. | `docs/coding_protocol.md` (lifecycle stage) | Journeys with no stated link to service are coded `personal_travel`; results for H2 are repeated with these journeys included. |
| A05 | Journeys that leave the orientation camp at the end of the course, including journeys to the home state before reporting at the place of primary assignment, are coded `travel_to_ppa`, and the travellers are coded `serving` because they have been sworn in. | Several reports describe sworn-in corps members abducted on the way home from camp; the protocol has no separate code for this leg, and it is closest to travel from camp to the posting. | `data/interim/incidents_working.csv`; `docs/coding_protocol.md` (lifecycle stage) | These journeys fall about three weeks after camp opening, outside the H2 windows around camp-opening and passing-out dates, so they count as incidents outside the windows. A version of H2 excluding them is reported as exploratory. |
| A06 | Harm to corps members on election duty as INEC ad hoc staff in their state of service is coded `ppa`. | Election duty takes place in the host community during the service year; the protocol has no separate code for it. | `data/interim/incidents_working.csv` | Election-duty incidents can be separated in exploratory analysis using the coding note. |
