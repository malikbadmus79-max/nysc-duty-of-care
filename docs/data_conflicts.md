# Data conflicts

Log of cases where two sources report different values for the same metric and period, with the resolution applied in the analysis.

| ID | Metric | Period | Value A (source) | Value B (source) | Difference | Resolution |
|----|--------|--------|------------------|------------------|------------|------------|
| C01 | Date of Federal Executive Council approval of the NYSC reform package | June 2026 | 24 June 2026 (Wednesday) [S04] | Monday before 30 June 2026, which is 29 June 2026 [S05] | 5 days | Resolved. The Federal Ministry of Youth Development statement of 29 June 2026 reports the approval as made that day [S21]. 29 June 2026 is used. |
| C02 | Corps members abducted in Ofu LGA, Kogi State | 26 August 2026 | 15 [S18] | 16 [S19] | 1 | S18 was published two days after the abduction, before the rescue; S19 reports the NYSC count at the rescue. The NYSC count of 16 is used when the incident enters the register, and both values are kept in the source notes. |
| C03 | Date of abduction of prospective corps members at Umunoha, Mbaitoli LGA, Imo State | October 2026 | 1 October 2026, about 6:30 am, police statement [S44] | 2 October 2026, about 4:30 am [S45] | 1 day | S44 was published on 1 October 2026 and reports a rescue on the same day, which rules out 2 October. 1 October 2026 is used. |
| C04 | Prospective corps members abducted at Umunoha, Mbaitoli LGA, Imo State | October 2026 | 20 [S43] | 19 [S45] | 1 | Police stated the exact number was being verified [S44]. 20 is used provisionally; the value is revised when an official count is published. |
| C05 | Date of abduction of corps members on the Ifon-Owo road, Ose LGA, Ondo State | July 2024 | Night of Friday 19 July 2024 [S38] | 23 July 2024 [S39] | 4 days | S38 was published on 20 July 2024 and reports the attack as already having happened; S39 gives its own publication date. 19 July 2024 is used. |
| C06 | Corps members killed in a bus crash in Kankara LGA, Katsina State | August 2019 | 3 [S82] | 2 [S83] | 1 | S82 gives the number of deaths and injuries in full (3 killed, 11 injured); 3 is used. |
| C07 | Manner of release of a corps member abducted in Jos North LGA, Plateau State | September 2019 | Freed in a security operation, police account [S85] | Freed after payment of N600,000, security sources [S85] | Not numeric | Outcome coded `released` with `ransom_reported` = yes, since a payment is reported; both accounts are kept in the coding note. |
