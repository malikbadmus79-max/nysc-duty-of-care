# Data conflicts

Log of cases where two sources report different values for the same metric and period, with the resolution applied in the analysis.

| C01 | Date of Federal Executive Council approval of the NYSC reform package | June 2026 | 24 June 2026 (Wednesday) [S04] | Monday before 30 June 2026, which is 29 June 2026 [S05] | 5 days | Resolved. The Federal Ministry of Youth Development statement of 29 June 2026 reports the approval as made that day [S21]. 29 June 2026 is used. |

| C04 | Prospective corps members abducted at Umunoha, Mbaitoli LGA, Imo State | October 2026 | 20 [S43] | 19 [S45] | 1 | Police stated the exact number was being verified [S44]. 20 is used provisionally; the value is revised when an official count is published. |
